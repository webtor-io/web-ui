Transfer status: рой ▸ кэш ▸ вы

Where the viewer's bytes come from and what caps them — the chain, the piece bar and the honest upsell. Approved design, 2026-09-24; revised 2026-09-25: the chain only while something moves, only its participants; otherwise the old badge.

Open after npm run build (loads assets/dist/style.css). Sample copy is Russian — the design was approved in ru.

A living reference: every block below is drawn by the component's real classes (.tx in assets/src/styles/style.css) and nothing on this page styles it. Implementation: templates/partials/resource/status.html renders the block from the server's view (services/statusview), assets/src/js/lib/transferStatus.js updates it in place from the status stream. Each block holds both the chain and the badge in one row (.tx-head); data-mode says which is shown. The page's own blocks carry data-* hooks and the details popover; these carry only what the look needs.

The design as the owner approved it (2026-09-24, revised 2026-09-25), with its own reference CSS, is kept frozen next to this page: transfer_status.approved.html. Compare against that, not against this page — this one is drawn by the implementation. Rows added here after the approval say so.

2026-09-25: the chain's middle node is «Кэш» (en “Cache”), no longer «Webtor» — the approved snapshot keeps the old label; a Vault transfer is Vault's own node with its share; the availability hint says «N% раздачи», not «файла».

Placement: film card

Desktop, container ~900px. The status block replaces the badge row and the piece-bar slot.

Rules

  • Placement. Inside the film card, right column, where the old badge row and the hairline piece-bar slot were: between the description and the action row. Phones (<640px): the block spans the whole card under the poster row — the column is ~215px and the chain does not fit. Below 670px of the block's own width (the longest chain, “missing” with three-digit peers in de/es/pt, needs 665px) the compact chain takes over; the swarm's value gives way with an ellipsis where even that is not enough.
  • One component. The chain or the badge → piece bar → hint or plan box. The sticky bar mirrors exactly this block on scroll; no compact variant. No torrent name in the status or the sticky bar.
  • Chain only while something moves. The chain is drawn while the swarm sends to the cache (caching, or into Vault, at a rate a label shows), or while a request of the viewer's is open — bytes going to them, a stall, pieces nobody has, or no number yet. Otherwise the old badge of the state, with the piece bar and the state's hint under it. The sticky bar and the details popover exist only with the chain.
  • Only participants. The chain draws who takes part: only caching — “Рой ▸ Кэш”; from the cache or Vault — “Кэш ▸ Вы” / “Vault ▸ Вы”; both — the whole chain. The source node is always there: «Кэш» (not the brand, 2026-09-25) with its share of the torrent while caching, the green check once all of it is there (no word next to it; the details' row says 100%); Vault's node with Vault's share while the torrent goes into Vault — with the viewer downloading too. No data from the proxy — “Вы” is not drawn. Vault waiting for seeders or retrying while the viewer is on the chain: “Кэш ▸ Вы” — Vault has stored nothing it could send; what it is doing stays the hint's. Nothing cached yet and a request of the viewer's open: “Кэш ▸ Вы” with no word next to the cache — «ожидает» there read as nobody being around, right next to «Вы». In the details, Vault's row during a Vault transfer says «что уже сохранено в Vault», never «сиды не нужны» while the swarm still sends the rest (that is for “saved” only).
  • Transitions. The swarm moves while its pieces arrive — not while a smoothed speed decays after them — and pieces come in bursts, so for the 10 s after its last piece it stays drawn as it last moved: its last speed, the sweep, the state that went with it; never a pause or a dash on the chain (those are the badge's). The viewer is on the chain by the proxy's word on their requests, not by speed (2026-09-25): while the proxy saw a request of theirs open since its previous one-second event — a quick HLS segment that opened and closed between two events included (its active; the count of requests open at the moment of the event alone missed every segment of the page's own player on a fast link, and “Вы” never came) — and until the proxy has seen none for 2 s; the speed is only the number on their segment. A gap in their requests shorter than 2 s never drops them (a download manager swapping one range request for the next); one of 3 s or more does, 2–3 s after their last request closed, whatever bytes its window still carries. A proxy too old to say falls back to its window's bytes: the viewer then stays until that five-second window has emptied, and the 2 s after it. When they leave and nothing else moves, the badge comes at once (2–3 s after the proxy sees the request close, not ~25 s). An HLS player has no request open between most of its segments: while the page's own player plays, waits for data or keeps buffering on pause, the page keeps them on the chain at their last reading (the server sends that view next to its own); paused with the buffer full — the proxy's view at once (“still buffering” is measured from the buffer at the pause, not from before the film started): the badge once the proxy sees no request of theirs. Not yet while the transcoder is still making the film: the paused player keeps reloading its playlist every ~2 s, the proxy counts each reload as a request, and the viewer stays on the chain until the proxy leaves playlists out of its counts (known, 2026-09-25). The viewer is also held for 10 s through a lost stream to the proxy while it is being reopened (a gap in the data, not a departure), and their last reading survives the reopen, so the page's playing player keeps them on the chain across it. Badge → chain at once. The plan's cap in two steps (2026-09-25; it used to be one, ~20 s): the pink “5 Мбит/с · потолок” on the viewer's link after 3 of the proxy's one-second events in a row at the cap, off at once when the proxy stops seeing it (throttled < 0.3 or under 0.7 of the cap); the plan box only after 8 s of a steady cap, and once up it goes only 10 s after the cap was last seen — through a dip under the cap it stays (the link shows the speed as it is), so the ~80px box never comes and goes with a slow second. A small file's few seconds at the cap show the pink link and no box. Both come up only on the proxy's word that the cap binds now: over its whole five-second window — never on the first seconds of a new stream to the proxy (every token rotation, every reopen, a web-ui rollout), whose average over 1–4 s reads a player's segment fetched at the cap as the cap — and, for the box, with a request of theirs coming, not on the window's tail after the download closed. The box, besides, rises only on an event with a request of the viewer's open at the proxy's sample (conns) — not on the one that sees their last request close, which raised a box that went two seconds later with them (on the recorded capped streams the gate cost no event). Before the box is due nothing stands under the bar in its place for a download or a player buffering or playing a file over the cap; a player playing a file of unknown bitrate gets the fact line, one playing a file under the cap nothing at all, and one buffering a file under the cap the cap line, with the pink link and gone with it (none of these ever gets a box). The badge and the chain share one row (both 24px tall), so switching never moves the card; focus on the chain or in its details moves to the badge and back.
  • Pieces nobody has. Once the seeder knows the connected peers' pieces, the bar hatches what nobody connected has — a red-tinted 135° hatch, one layer under the whole bar shown over the missing cells so its stripes run on across a run of cells, completion drawn over it — in either mode. With peers but no seeder: the viewer waiting on such a piece — “missing” on the chain; nothing moving — the “needed pieces missing” badge; Vault waiting — “waiting for missing pieces”. Also before a single piece is cached (0%), and not in the first seconds of a status stream, before the swarm has had the time to show it moves. The hint says the cause and, where Vault is configured, offers to save the torrent to Vault. The percent and the holes are the whole torrent's — every file of it, not only the page's file (the status follows the torrent): the hint says «в рое есть N% раздачи», never «файла».
  • Direction. Left to right, towards the viewer. Never ↑ for “to you”: on a torrent site ↑ next to “you” reads as “you are seeding”, and ↑ is already the Vault cloud icon.
  • Units. Mbit/s everywhere (plans, speedtest and limit copy already use it). “≈ MB/s” only in the details popover.
  • Colour means cause. Cyan — data flowing; pink — the plan cap (the only place a CTA appears); amber — the swarm or a pause; purple — Vault; red — no seeders.
  • Motion. One sweep per segment: a single line under the speed label, a 30%-wide gradient moving background-position −43% → 143% in 1.8s, linear. The label and the arrowhead only brighten slightly (filter: brightness(1.18)) at 50% and ~90% of the cycle — no glow. prefers-reduced-motion: no sweep, the line is simply filled; nothing pulses, and “checking” shows its three dots still.
  • Accessibility. The chain is the button that opens the details; its name is fixed (“Где узкое место”), its description is the badge's words — the state as text, which the badge cannot say while the chain is up.
  • Stable DOM. Update text and classes in place on each SSE message; re-creating nodes restarts every animation once a second and drops clicks on the CTA. The chain and the badge are both in the block for good.
  • Honest CTA. Plan box = bolt + title + ETA on the left, button with the trial note under it on the right (the download_file.html pattern). Only with the chain, only at the cap — and only once the cap has held for 8 s (the pink link says it first); held 10 s past the cap, never past an honesty rule below. Download: “до N раз быстрее” while caching, “в N раз быстрее” for cached/Vault. Stream (2026-09-26): the bitrate known and above the cap (the stream job marks the player data-status-over-cap) — the stream box as soon as it is due, while the player still plays from its buffer (stream_over): at the cap the stall is certain, and a grace buffer hides it for minutes; the bitrate known and within the cap with room to spare (1.2×, statusview.FitsMargin: The Knick, estimated at 0.87 of the cap, pulled 1.01 of it in TS segments and stalled four times in 180 s) — no line at all while it plays; the bitrate unknown or within that margin — the fact while it plays; either way the box at the first real buffering once it is due, since a stall while thp’s limiter holds the viewer is the cap’s doing whatever the estimate said (a real buffering: a waiting that lasts 1.5 s after it played; Chrome's timeupdate right after waiting, the clock where it stopped, is the stall reported, not playback; a stall whose player left the page — another file picked, “Next” — ends with it, counted as far as it was seen). The bitrate is the stream's, not the file's (jobs/scripts playedBitrate): the player pulls one video and one audio track — the transcoder copies H.264 and serves each dub as its own stereo AAC rendition, nginx-vod repackages an mp4's first tracks — while the file's rate counts every dub (of 202 H.264 files over a 5 Mbps cap by the file, 36 are under it as a stream). Unknown, so marked neither way: video the transcoder re-encodes (its rate is the encoder's choice), no per-track number, or statistics tags a later remux left stale. Nothing is sold while the page's player is inside its free grace window by movie time (the element's clock plus its session's offset: after a session seek it counts from the seek point) — the proxy's verdict can be on there, from the segments hls.js fetches ahead past the window at the cap. Leaving the window, the grace popup is the offer: from the moment the element crosses until the player puts the popup up (a frame or more later; never while the tab is hidden) it counts as on screen, so the status says the line, not a box the popup would fold into a line a moment later. Answered, the popup has said it (owner, 2026-09-26): once the viewer presses “Continue at N Mbps” or closes it (the player marks the element data-grace-cta-answered) — or chose “watch as is” on the slow-download modal before playback (its force-slow run renders data-offer-answered), they have just been told the cap is coming — a file over the cap gets no box and no line (not the fact: “no stops” is false for it; the pink link stays) until the player's first real stall, then the stream box as at any capped stall (the server's timing unchanged: the pink link after 3 events, the box 8 s after the cap, gone 10 s after it), and the over-cap rule from then on. A stall under way when they answer counts — the video is frozen at the cap now; one that ended before the answer was the popup's. Since the popup stops the film (2026-09-26) its pause ends any stall before the answer, so the first that counts is the resumed film's, once the buffer it grew at the cap under the popup runs out; the popup's pause is the page's, not the viewer's — the element carries data-grace-cta-hold while the popup holds playback and the status reads it as playing: the viewer stays on the chain, no minute runs out. Before this the box came ~0.5 s after the popup closed (thp binds on the segments past the window). Files of unknown bitrate or under the cap keep their rules; no popup at all (paid tiers, grace off) — no answer, the box once due. The mark lives on the element: the next file, a reload, another grace window start without it. No CTA on pause, no/few seeders, missing pieces, stall, Vault failure — a held box goes at once there too. Hints state only the cause — never what a subscription would or would not do. Anonymous viewers: “Без подписки — до 5 Мбит/с”, never “your plan”.
